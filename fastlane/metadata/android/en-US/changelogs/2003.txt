Hatch Rush has a race again. The best run was filed under how many eggs
that round happened to hold, but rounds vary from ten to sixteen — so
almost every run found an empty slot, raced nobody, and was greeted as
a first rush. Best runs are now kept as a pace, and the ghost stretches
to fit whatever round comes up.

Quickest is decided per egg rather than per round, so a longer round no
longer loses just for being longer. Times already set are kept.
