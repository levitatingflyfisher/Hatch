Sound works. Every cue had been silently muted since audio landed — the
mute flag never reached the player. Plinks, snaps and the hatch chirps
are all audible now.

Nothing is snatched away any more. A filled tray settles before the
question arrives, a correct sweep holds for a beat, and a wrong answer
stops: the tray shows what went short or spilled over, the fact says
what it is, and it waits for a tap. Stages fade into one another
instead of cutting.
