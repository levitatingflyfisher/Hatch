# 0005 — Fold the fact space; mirrors plant, never auto-fire

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-08-06

## Context

Commutativity halves the multiplication table — 66 genuinely distinct facts,
not 121 — and making that visible is one of the strongest motivational moves
available (the unknown region shrinks toward a small corner). The tempting
implementation is to credit 8×3 the moment 3×8 masters. But retrieval is
cue-specific: children demonstrably stall on the mirrored prompt of a fact
they "know," and silently crediting untested items falsifies the Album
(the child's view and the truth must be the same picture).

## Decision

The engine folds the space (canonical a ≤ b) but tracks retrieval
**per direction**. Mastering a×b auto-**plants** its mirror — the mirrored
prompt enters rotation at the labeled rung, taught once by the rotate-lock
vignette — and the twin pair shows *hatched* on the Album only after at
least one confirmed fast production of the mirrored direction. Hatch Rush
alternates directions as a matter of round assembly.

## Consequences

- The Album's twin-hatch moment stays honest and still arrives fast (one
  confirmation, not a full ladder).
- Engine law 4; the rotate-lock puzzle is the only place commutativity is
  "explained," and it is wordless.
