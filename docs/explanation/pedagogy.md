# The pedagogy

What Hatch teaches, in what order, and why — with the evidence stated at
its honest strength. (Primary sources: Kling & Bay-Williams' NCTM fact-fluency
work; Baroody's phase model; the Singapore/Bruner CPA literature; Math
Academy's published pedagogy; the spacing/retrieval meta-analytic literature;
Habgood & Ainsworth on intrinsic integration.)

## The thesis

The curriculum for multiplication facts is a solved problem; the software's
job is motivation and scheduling. Hatch invents no pedagogy — it encodes
the practitioner-consensus sequence and the cognitive-science scheduling that
classrooms struggle to deliver per-child, and wraps them in a game whose
mechanics *are* the mathematics.

## The sequence

Foundational families first — **×2, ×10, ×5** (skip-counting anchors), then
**×1/×0** (one-lesson rules), then the **squares** (landmark facts on the
diagonal). Everything else is *derived* from what the child now owns:

| Family | Strategy | In the toy |
|---|---|---|
| ×4 | double the ×2 | FOLD the ×2 tray |
| ×3 | ×2 plus one group | STACK one more row of eggs |
| ×9 | ×10 minus one group | SLICE the extra row off the ×10 tray |
| ×6 | double ×3, or ×5 plus a group | FOLD or STACK — child's choice |
| ×7 | split at the five-anchor (5+2) | SLICE into 5×n and 2×n, SNAP back |
| ×8 | double the ×4 | FOLD again |

Commutativity halves the space (66 distinct facts); the residue genuinely new
by the ×6/×7/×8 families is small, and the Album makes that visible.

This is Baroody's arc made mandatory: **counting → deriving → automaticity**.
Traditional drill fails by jumping phase 1 → 3; the toy makes phase 2
unskippable, because the derivation *is* the manipulation that builds the
tray. Strategy-plus-practice beats drill-alone on transfer and maintenance
in controlled comparisons; drill still contributes automaticity — so
Hatch does both, in that order.

## The weaning ladder (CPA)

Each fact climbs four rungs — open tray (every egg countable) → carton-bundled
rows (counting-all physically removed; skip-counting forced) → closed labeled
tray (dimensions only; derived moves) → bare tag (pure symbol). Symbols ride
every tray from rung 1 ("symbolic develops along the way" — the CPA
literature's correction to the strict-stages misreading), and a miss at the
bare rung opens the tray back to imagery — pictures as the fallback when
symbols fail, per Bruner. The ladder is per-fact: a child is rung 4 on her 2s
while rung 1 on her 7s. Both named CPA failure modes — lingering in pictures,
rushing to symbols — are handled by the same mechanism, automatically.

## Scheduling

Mastery gating (a family opens when its strategy source is ~80% automatic),
expanding-interval spaced retrieval on a **calendar-day clock** (1→3→7→14→30
days, per-fact speed multiplier), review rounds interleaved across all open
families, misses closing only by re-retrieval, and placement probes so a child
who already knows facts reaches her true frontier in session one. The
automaticity criterion and the timed-practice question are ADR-0004; the
commutativity rule is ADR-0005.

## What the evidence supports — and what it doesn't

- **Spacing**: meta-analytic g ≈ 0.28 for math; expanding intervals beat equal
  spacing for children's generalization. Solid; load-bearing.
- **Mastery gating + interleaving**: the Math Academy/Bloom lineage; strong
  convergent support, no large RCT of this exact configuration.
- **Intrinsic integration**: in the best experiments, math-in-the-mechanic
  beat identical-math-as-quiz on delayed tests (d ≈ 0.74 in one; 58% vs 41%
  in another) and children chose it 7× longer in free play. Real, replicated
  twice, small samples.
- **Retrieval practice in math specifically**: weaker than its reputation
  (g ≈ 0.18, CI crossing zero, 7 studies). We treat retrieval as the delivery
  vehicle and lean on spacing for the durability claim.
- **Scale honesty**: the closest shipped intrinsic exemplar (ST Math, 16,307
  students, cluster-randomized) moved state scores < 0.1 SD. Expect a
  well-taught fact course delivered kindly — not a miracle.
