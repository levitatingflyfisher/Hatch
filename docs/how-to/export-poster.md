# Export an album poster

The poster is Hatch's "show grandma" artifact: the child's whole 11×11
album — every hatched critter in color, every waiting egg pale and patient —
as one printable page with her name and the date.

## From the Album

1. Open **The Album**.
2. Tap the share icon in the top corner.
3. The system share sheet opens with two files: a **PNG** (2048×2048, good
   for messages and screens) and a **PDF** (A4, good for printing). Send
   them wherever the share sheet reaches — a printer, a chat, a drive.

The same action is available from the parent corner.

## What the poster shows — and doesn't

- Hatched critters appear in full color; everything else is an egg. The
  poster celebrates what's done and never marks what's "due" — sleepy
  states, review schedules, and speed exist nowhere on it.
- Rendering happens entirely on-device (the app has no network permission);
  the only copy that leaves is the one the parent explicitly shares, and
  where it goes is the operating system's choice, not the app's.

## On the web app

Browsers without a native share target download the two files instead —
same content, same names (`hatch-album-<date>.png` / `.pdf`).
