# Her first session

Ten minutes, start to finish. This walks a parent through what the first
session actually looks like — hand her the device and read along.

## 1. Lay an egg (make a hatcher)

Open Hatch. Tap the big egg to add a **hatcher** — pick one of the egg
patterns, type a name (or don't; "Hatcher 1" is a fine name). No email, no
password, no account: the profile lives only on this device.

## 2. The home screen

Four doors: **The Nursery** (marked *start here* on a brand-new hatcher),
**Hatch Rush**, **The Album**, and **Habitats**. The two small icons up top —
the parent corner and settings — are for you, not her.

## 3. The Nursery — the first round

Rounds are short: a handful of eggs along the top show how far along she is,
and a round is over in a few minutes.

- The first prompts size her up. Hatch starts everyone gently and moves the
  frontier to wherever she actually is — a child who already knows her ×5s
  won't be made to crawl through them. Nothing is labelled a "test."
- **Building**: she fills an incubator tray with rows of eggs — drag a row
  in, or tap-tap to stack them. A 3×4 tray *is* 3×4; the running count says
  so out loud. Then the tray asks: *how many?* — she answers on the big
  number pad.
- **A wrong answer is the best part.** Her number renders as real eggs
  against the true tray: too few leaves empty wells blinking, too many
  spills over the edge, and the tray shows exactly which "one more row"
  fixes it. The sound is the softest in the app. The same fact returns
  before the round ends.
- **Ghost lessons**: now and then a little ghost cursor plays through a
  trick using trays she already owns — *double it!*, *take one row away* —
  then hands her the same move. These are the times-table strategies,
  taught wordlessly.

## 4. The first hatch

When a fact has been answered quickly and correctly on several different
days, its egg cracks — a critter pops out, chirps, and moves into the
Album for good. (So no, nothing hatches in session one. The first hatch
usually lands in week one, and it means something *because* it took days —
that's the memory science, not a slow drip.)

## 5. The other doors

- **Hatch Rush** opens after a bit of Nursery practice: a sub-minute run
  racing the ghost caterpillar of her own best — never a clock, never
  another child.
- **The Album** shows the whole times table from minute one: hatched
  critters in color, everything else an egg, honestly waiting. Sleepy critters want a
  quick visit — that's the review schedule, wearing a costume.
- **Habitats** is hers: arrange hatched critters in a meadow, a pond, a
  hill. Nothing is scored there.

## 6. For you, after

Hold the parent-corner button for two seconds (a child-speed-bump, not a
lock): the same album as a heatmap, plain-language progress, and the
**album poster** — a printable page of every critter so far, for the
refrigerator or grandma. See
[export a poster](../how-to/export-poster.md) and
[back up her progress](../how-to/backup-restore.md).

There is no step 7. No daily reminder will chase her; the app can't send
one. Leave it where she can find it.
